Torn parchment — where the building type goes

Same tooltip, four placements for the category. Guild (utility, short) and Mill (utility, longest in the catalogue) in each.

1Eyebrow above the name

Reads top-down: what kind, what it's called, what it does. Costs one line of height on every tooltip.

2After the name, same line

No extra height at all. Long names plus a long category get close to the right edge — "Chapter House · Utility" is the worst case.

3Right-aligned on the name's line

No extra height, and name and category can never collide because they grow from opposite edges. Scans well when several tooltips are seen in a session.

4Footer under the rule

Most formal, and the rule gives the parchment something to hold. Costs a line and a rule on every tooltip, which is a lot of furniture for one word.
