Choosing

Design 2 is correct for every position that exists today and reads better at the moment of decision, but its hire row doubles and Ordination would eventually force a special case. Design 3 keeps the hire question exactly as it is and handles Ordination for free, at the price of a line that updates.

If Ordination is going to gain a real Bank-and-hire position, design 3 is the one that will still be right then. If it is not, design 2 is cleaner.
