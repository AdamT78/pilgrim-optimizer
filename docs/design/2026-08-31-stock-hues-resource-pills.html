Stocks that read as themselves

The three readouts, their rules, their glyphs and their hidden choice keys are lifted straight out of the served page — same geometry, same parchment (#E4D9BC), same amounts. The only thing that changes across these panels is the fill and stroke of the choice key.

What is true today

All three glyphs are drawn in one ink, #3A2F1E, and all three amounts in the same. Nothing on the board distinguishes wheat from stone from silver except the shape of the glyph.

The choice key is struck in render_player_boards_v2.py:_render_resource_choice_keys as fill=surface_background, stroke=_darker_surface_colour(...) — #E4D9BC on #A49C87, identical for all three. resource_choice_styles() only reveals and hides, and says so: “no fill is named here or anywhere the script can reach.” So per-resource colour has to come from the renderer. That is the whole shape of the change: one dict in Python, no CSS, no client.

Real states below. After hiring the Bank: cost 2, silver the only payable stock. After declining it: cost 1, wheat the only one. Red holds 1 wheat, 0 stone, 2 silver.

Hue carries the resource, treatment carries the state

3

Three stocks × three states

Live — payable now

All three shown together as a specimen, not a state — this scenario never offers stone. 2px stroke in the stock’s own hue, a wash of it for fill.

Spent — already paid

Silver at 0 after paying twice. This is the ‘spent’ state the payment prototypes already carry in their legend, drained of hue and dropped to a 1px keyline rather than tinted. The stock is still marked as the one that paid; it is simply no longer a key.

Not offered

No key, the rules between the readouts stay up, and the stock is a readout again — which is what the live page already does. Absence here is safe because the number never goes away.

wheat  #9A6B12 on #F3E3AF
stone  #5A5751 on #DCD8CE
silver  #3F6E93 on #D5E4EF

Gold, neutral and steel: one warm, one hueless, one cool, so the three separate for a red-green colour-blind reader as well — the distinction that matters is not carried by red against green anywhere in this set.

A variant, and the limits

4

Should the amount take the hue too?

Pill only (recommended)

Colour appears only while the stock is a key, so the resting board is untouched everywhere else in the app. One function changes.

Pill and numeral

The amount picks up the stroke colour while the choice is live. Louder, and arguably clearer at a distance — but it re-inks a numeral the rest of the board draws in #3A2F1E, so the board is no longer one typographic system.

What this does not do. The resting board is unchanged: the glyphs stay in #3A2F1E and the readouts look exactly as they do now until something asks a question. Giving the stocks a permanent colour identity is a larger change to the boards and is not proposed here.

Where it lands. _render_resource_choice_keys gains a per-stock (fill, stroke) lookup and strikes it in place of surface_background. resource_choice_styles() is not touched, the client is not touched, and the reveal/hide contract holds: the script still names no fill.

Open. Piety has its own pill (data-piety-choice-pill) and is not in this set. If stocks take hues, piety needs a fourth or an explicit decision to stay neutral.
