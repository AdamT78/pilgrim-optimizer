Pay when you hire

The hire button names no resource; the payment is asked immediately after it, and the acolyte arrangement comes last. No standing line, no rewritten text, no new panel row — and, as it turns out, no new step order either. 5

Against the other two

No standing line, no text that changes after it was settled, no new panel row, and no growing button list. Each of the earlier designs needed at least one of those.
The Bank needs no question and no wording. It is one more lit stock at a payment that should have existed anyway.
Ordination needs no special case. Wherever a payment is asked, the Bank is one of the stocks that may answer it, however many payments a turn has.
The work is still the 151 fixed-resource hires that have no payment step — 106 with none at all, 33 with it behind the arrangement, plus the smaller shapes. Every one changes: a step appears, and Confirm stops being live until it is answered. That is the part to verify by applying actions and diffing resources, not by reading labels.
