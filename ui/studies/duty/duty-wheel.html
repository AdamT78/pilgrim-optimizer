The rail — two variants

Design 2 kept, with the real action sets and the real tithe counters from one generated game (2 players, seed 118). The two variants differ in where the Tithe goes.

Actions per duty are _DUTY_CATEGORY_ACTIONS. Tithe counters and duty positions are the generator's own output for that seed. Ink language, seal states, cube tally and rail geometry are unchanged from the design you picked.

One correction that shapes both variants. The tithe counter is not a property of a duty — it belongs to the board position, and the tiles are shuffled onto the positions at setup. The pool is exactly seven (stone, stone, wheat, wheat, silver, silver, cornucopia) for eight spaces, and _generate_tithe_counters deals them to every position except wherever Taxation lands. So one tile every game has no tithe at all, and in this game Clerical's tithe is stone rather than silver, and Construct's is the cornucopia wild.
