Four seasons, and how far the next end is

Level has moved from the fill to the outline, so the field is free for the seat wash: Chapel is blue's, Library red's, Bank white's. Mint is donated — still yellow's, usable by nobody, hireable by nobody — so it goes grey and its round, which can never come again, is replaced by the word. The board is on round 12 and the next season end is round 13.

C1 — a caption under each season

The countdown is written out, so it survives the fact that the ribbon SKIPS the ten empty rounds — you cannot count hexes to the next end, because hexes are not rounds. Past seasons say scored; the rest say when they land. Hex radius 40.8 px.

C2 — the countdown on the tile itself

No caption row, so the hexes keep their size and the next season end carries the number in place of its round. Only one tile can say it, and only the next one. Hex radius 43.9 px.

Counted in rounds, which is what the ship moves in and what the track is made of. `configs/timing.json` puts `players_per_round` at 2, so if you want player-turns instead it is this number times that, and the unit is a one-word change.
