The sample beside each option is the same six tiles every time — Chapel (blue), Library (red), Bank (white), Mint (yellow, and donated), and Brewery, which is the round the board is on.

C2, with the four changes

Black borders throughout, Season End keeps its own fill, the current round (12, Brewery) is the only green number, and Donated sits under the name. Ownership shown here with the seat cube.

Five ways to say who owns it

Seat cube

The game's own token, laid on the tile. Every other surface — player boards, duty tiles, the city, special activities — already says seat with a cube of exactly this shape, so nothing new has to be learned.

Seat border

The outline takes the colour instead of black. Unmissable at a glance across the whole strip, and it costs no room inside the tile — but it is the channel the level colour just vacated, so it cannot come back later.

Foot band

A bar along the bottom edge, clear of both the name and the round. Closest to the card's own top-band gradient, turned the right way up for a hex.

Corner flag

One corner of the hex filled in. Small, quiet, and it survives being half-covered by the neighbouring hex in the ribbon — which the cube does not always.

Flat tint

The gradient's honest version: the whole field, one flat pass of the seat colour. White is the one that struggles, on parchment.

Two things the black border costs, worth deciding on rather than inheriting: the level is now nowhere on the tile at all, and the round box no longer says whether a building has woken — green now means this is the round we are on, so past and future rounds read the same.
