Does the market need the level colours?

Four buildings are owned, one per seat — Chapel by blue, Library by red, Mint by yellow, Bank by white — drawn as the README's convention has it, a seat wash down from the top edge. The round number is boxed green once woken and grey while it is still coming. Same 1193 × 127.

1 — level in the fill (what the sheet does now)

Blue's wash on Chapel and red's on Library are the two the level fill swallows: a seat colour laid over a fill of its own hue stops reading as a seat colour.

2 — level in the outline, the fill left free

The hue survives, so the market and the map still agree, but it is a line rather than a field — and the field is left to the wash, which is the only thing that needs one.

3 — no level colour, and no level

One parchment for everything. All four washes are unmistakable. Nothing says where level 2 stops and level 3 starts.

4 — no level colour, level as the numeral

The same, with the numeral the tile label used to be headed by brought back, small, above the round. Level stays sayable without spending a colour on it.

Levels always run 1, 2, 3 along the track — `build_abstract_setup_timeline` gates each level behind a pilgrimage site and its cursor only moves forward — so the order carries the level on its own. What the order does not show is where one level stops: the round-13 season end falls inside level 2, so Reliquary on 15 is still level 2 and Bank on 18 is the first level 3.
