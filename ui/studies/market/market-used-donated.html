Used and donated are not the same kind of fact

Used is cleared by advance_turn on every turn boundary and is keyed by building rather than by player — so it means spent for this turn, by whoever spent it, and it comes back next turn. Donated never comes back. One is a thing laid on a tile and taken off again; the other is what the tile now is. Giving both the same grey says they are the same, and they are not.

Every strip below: Guild is used and unowned (hired off the map), Library is used and red's (either red used it free or someone hired it from red — the tile does not care, and neither does the rule), Indulgences is blue's and available, Bank is white's, Mint is yellow's and donated, Brewery is the current round. The foot bar never changes: ownership is not a state either of these touches.

Hatch — ruled over

Thin lines laid across the tile. Everything underneath stays fully legible, and the mark reads as something drawn ON the tile rather than a change to it — which is what a state that clears at the end of the turn should look like.

Veil — in shadow

One flat pass of dark. Quieter than the hatch and unmistakable at strip scale, but it is the same gesture as the donated grey, only darker: a change to the field.

Chip — something laid on it

A token dropped on the tile, the way you would cover a space at the table. The strongest signal that it comes off again, and the only one that costs the tile any room.

Pale ink — the writing fades, the paper does not

The mirror of donated: donated changes the field, used changes the ink. Two durations, two channels, and no shade of grey has to mean two things.

Whatever wins, the donated tile keeps the grey field and the word, because that is a permanent condition of the building and deserves the permanent-looking channel.
