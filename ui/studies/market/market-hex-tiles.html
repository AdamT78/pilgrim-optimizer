The market, as hex tiles

The twelve building cards become the twelve building tiles, and the four pilgrimage sites join them. Every name, level and round is the same seeded setup the map is drawn from (seed 20260730), so a tile here is the same tile on the map's edge. Each frame is the 1193 × 127 the current strip occupies, so a chosen variant drops straight in.

A — the round track unrolled

Sixteen hexes interlocked the way they sit on the map's edge, in round order. The four season ends break the ribbon by themselves, so nothing else has to mark one. Hex radius 48.7 px, name 13.1 px.

B — a straight row

Flat-top hexes cannot interlock along a row, so they meet point to point. Reads as beads rather than as a track, and the name pays for it. Hex radius 35.1 px, name 9.4 px.

C — four seasons, broken after each end

A's ribbon cut after each season end. The break is explicit; the tiles pay a little size for it. Hex radius 43.9 px, name 11.8 px.

The board is on round 12: tiles later than that are dimmed because they have not woken yet, and Infirmary and Mint carry the seat that hired them. A season end is where the Alms Table scores and resets. The generator always puts site 1 on round 1, which is where the ship starts rather than a season ending, so that marker is shown at round 26 instead — the ship closing its lap of the map is the fourth season end and the end of the game.
