Market — every building in the catalogue

Two real generated games whose draws happen to share nothing, so between them all 24 buildings appear once. Top-band gradient for ownership, round number boxed.

Both markets come from generate_setup_scenario(2, seed) — the draw, the level split and every live round are the generator's own, and the rule text, names, stone costs and donation VP are verbatim from configs/buildings.json. Seeds 118 and 1086 were found by searching 2,599 generated setups for a pair with no building in common. Only who owns what is placed by hand, so that all four seat washes and the donated state appear in context.
