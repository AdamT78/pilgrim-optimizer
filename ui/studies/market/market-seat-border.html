How thin does the seat band have to be?

The band eats inwards, so every unit of it comes off the width the name has to live in. Indulgences is now blue's, so the worst case in the catalogue is inside a ring in every strip below. Library is red's, Bank white's, Mint yellow's and donated.

Or: let the one word that does not fit, fit

Band 5 again, with any name wider than its tile scaled down to it. Only Indulgences and Scriptorium in the whole catalogue ever trip it, so 22 of the 24 buildings are drawn at exactly the size they are now — and the band stops having to be a hairline to accommodate one word.

The bare hex offers 86.4 units at the name's depth and Indulgences wants 82.1 — 2.2 a side before any band exists at all. `render_building_tile` says as much in its own docstring: the widest word "fills all but 7 units of the hex at this depth". So thinning the band does not solve this so much as postpone it: only a hairline of 1.5 clears, and a hairline is a weak thing to hang ownership on — especially white's.
