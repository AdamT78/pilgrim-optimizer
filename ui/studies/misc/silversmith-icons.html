Silversmith — six candidates

Clerical's second action. Each glyph is drawn the way the tile's other action glyphs are built, so any of them drops straight in. The middle column is true size on the 3:2 canvas; the next one is the same drawing at 3×.

true size
3×
A · Chalice
What a silversmith makes for a church. The clearest silhouette of the set, and the only one that says silver rather than smith.
B · Anvil
The oldest trade sign there is. Reads as a smith of any metal, so it leans on the tile to supply the silver.
C · Hammer & coin
Builds on a glyph this UI already teaches -- the coin is the silver the player boards use -- with the hammer that works it.
D · Punch & die
Striking a blank: punch above, disc below. Names the act of working silver more exactly than a hammer alone.
E · Crucible
Pouring molten silver. The most period of the set, and the one that most needs a caption the first time.
F · Tongs
A shape nothing else on the wheel uses, so it survives being small -- but it reads as a shape before it reads as tongs.
