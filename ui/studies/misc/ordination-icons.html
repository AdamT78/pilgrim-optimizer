Ordination — the second action

An acolyte moving from the Abbey to the City. The first action — the serf into the Abbey — is the wheel's existing gable, shown above each candidate in the last column so you can judge the pair rather than the icon alone.

the pair
A · Abbey & arrow out
The wheel's existing idiom: Give Alms already says leave here with a building and an arrow. Cheap to learn, but it will look like that tile's icon at a glance.
B · Arrow into the city
Names the destination, which is the only thing that differs from the first action. The battlements are the one shape on the wheel that can only mean a town.
C · Acolyte & arrow
Names who moves rather than where to. Reads at any size and never collides with a building, but leaves the destination to the tile.
D · Abbey to city
Says both ends of the move and needs no context at all. Also the busiest of the five -- three objects in a box that renders 24 px across.
E · Spire & arrow
The city as one tower instead of a wall -- the same idea as B with a third of the strokes, at the cost of looking a little like the abbey it is not.
