reference image: donate building v1

Use the attached image as the primary reference for the central motif and use the previously established red wax seals as the structural reference for realism, shallow relief, lighting, thin perimeter, and small-size readability.

Create ONE hyper-realistic medieval RED WAX SEAL, isolated on a fully transparent background.

SUBJECT

The seal represents:

DONATE BUILDING

The central impression must show:

ONE OPEN HAND SUPPORTING ONE MEDIEVAL BUILDING.

The composition should clearly communicate:

A BUILDING BEING GIVEN / OFFERED / DONATED

rather than construction, ownership, protection, or possession.

The motif should be reminiscent of the attached reference:
- one large supporting hand below
- one complete building resting clearly in the palm
- the building centered above the hand
- simple, bold silhouettes
- clear separation between hand and building

The motif must remain readable when the seal is displayed at approximately 55–70 px wide.

======================================================================
BUILDING MOTIF
======================================================================

Show ONE medieval secular building resting in the open palm.

The building should feel appropriate to fourteenth-century England.

Good visual characteristics:

- compact rectangular structure
- steep pitched roof
- timber or stone construction translated into wax relief
- one clear doorway
- one or two simple windows
- perhaps a small chimney
- restrained medieval architectural character

Prefer a building that reads as:

HOUSE / HALL / STOREHOUSE / WORKSHOP / ALMS HOUSE

rather than a church.

Do NOT use:
- a cross on the roof
- a church tower
- a chapel silhouette
- a cathedral
- a castle
- a fortress
- battlements
- flags
- heraldic banners

The building must remain simple.

At small size it should read first as:

BUILDING

not as a detailed architectural illustration.

======================================================================
HAND MOTIF
======================================================================

Use ONE large open hand underneath the building.

The palm should face upward.

The building should appear to rest naturally and securely in the hand.

The hand should:

- extend horizontally across the lower half of the seal
- have a broad readable palm
- use simplified but believable fingers
- curve slightly upward at the fingertips
- feel supportive rather than grasping
- clearly communicate offering / donation

Do NOT show:
- two hands
- a closed fist
- fingers gripping the building
- hands dropping the building
- a pointing hand
- a disembodied hand floating far from the building

The relationship should immediately read as:

THIS BUILDING IS BEING GIVEN.

======================================================================
COMPOSITION
======================================================================

Keep the motif bold and uncluttered.

The full hand + building composition should occupy approximately 68–74% of the usable seal face.

Suggested hierarchy:

TOP / CENTER:
one medieval building

BOTTOM:
one broad open supporting hand

The building should occupy approximately 40–45% of the usable seal width.

The hand should occupy approximately 60–70% of the usable seal width.

The hand should visually support the building rather than overlap it excessively.

Keep generous negative space around the motif.

Do not add scenery.

Do not add ground.

Do not add surrounding architecture.

Do not add extra objects.

======================================================================
REAL WAX-SEAL CONSTRUCTION — VERY IMPORTANT
======================================================================

This must look like a genuine physical red wax seal created by pressing an engraved metal matrix into warm sealing wax.

The hand and building must be physically impressed into the wax as shallow bas-relief.

Everything must be made from the SAME RED WAX as the seal.

The building is NOT a separate miniature sitting on wax.

The hand is NOT a separate sculpted ornament.

The motif should have:

- shallow rounded relief
- softly compressed edges
- modest variation in impression depth
- broad readable planes
- shallow recesses separating hand from building
- subtle imperfections where the matrix did not press perfectly

The entire object must feel like one continuous wax impression.

Do NOT make the motif look glued onto the seal.
Do NOT make it look like a modern 3D icon.
Do NOT make it look printed.
Do NOT make it look like an enamel badge.

======================================================================
IMPORTANT — RELIEF DEPTH
======================================================================

Keep the relief comparatively shallow.

The hand and building should project only enough to create clear light and shadow.

Avoid exaggerated 3D depth.

Avoid deep undercuts.

Avoid making the house look like a miniature model physically sitting above the wax.

The entire design must remain unmistakably an impressed seal.

======================================================================
RED WAX MATERIAL
======================================================================

Use deep crimson / blood-red sealing wax.

Rich dark crimson across the seal face.

Use:

- slightly brighter crimson highlights on raised relief
- deeper burgundy in recesses
- restrained polished highlights
- subtle wax translucency only at very thin edges
- natural variation in gloss

The entire motif must remain red wax.

Do NOT introduce:
- brown timber
- gray stone
- gold
- blue
- green
- white
- metallic colors

All separation must come from:

RELIEF
LIGHT
SHADOW

rather than different colors.

======================================================================
VERY THIN OUTER EDGE
======================================================================

Keep the outer wax perimeter VERY THIN.

The central usable seal face should occupy approximately 88–92% of the overall diameter.

Around it, leave only a narrow irregular wax lip approximately 4–6% of the seal radius.

The edge should look like warm wax naturally displaced when the matrix was pressed.

It should be:

- thin
- organic
- slightly uneven
- gently flattened
- subtly wavy
- physically believable

NO thick fantasy rim.
NO scalloped medallion border.
NO decorative circular frame.
NO rope ring.
NO coin edge.
NO ornamental surround.

The silhouette should be nearly circular but naturally imperfect.

======================================================================
SMALL-SIZE READABILITY
======================================================================

This asset will often appear approximately 55–70 px wide.

Design specifically for this.

At thumbnail size the player should immediately perceive:

HAND + BUILDING

and therefore:

DONATE BUILDING

Use only a few strong shapes.

For the building:

- one clear roof
- one clear doorway
- one or two broad windows at most
- no tiny masonry
- no tiny roof tiles
- no elaborate timber framing

For the hand:

- broad palm
- clearly readable thumb
- simplified fingers
- no fine anatomical wrinkles

Avoid microscopic detail.

Avoid excessive decorative architecture.

Avoid thin engraved lines.

The silhouette must do most of the communication.

======================================================================
SURFACE / AGE
======================================================================

The seal should show restrained physical age:

- tiny pits
- faint scratches
- very small hairline cracks
- subtle compression marks
- slight variation in wax sheen
- mild edge wear

Keep damage restrained.

The seal should feel old and handled but intact.

Do not make it shattered or archaeological.

======================================================================
LIGHTING
======================================================================

Soft directional studio lighting from upper left.

Use the light to clarify the relief:

- soft highlights along upper-left roof edges
- gentle highlight across the top of the hand
- deeper burgundy where the building meets the palm
- slightly darker shadows beneath architectural projections
- soft shadow separation between fingers

No magical lighting.
No glow.
No firelight.
No colored reflections.
No external cast shadow.

======================================================================
CAMERA / PRESENTATION
======================================================================

Perfectly front-facing.

Orthographic / extremely long focal-length appearance.

No perspective distortion.

Seal centered.

Generous transparent space around the complete seal.

Fully transparent background outside the seal.

No black background.
No white background.
No parchment.
No tabletop.
No frame.
No external shadow.

Production-quality medieval dark-fantasy board-game UI asset.

======================================================================
EXCLUSIONS
======================================================================

NO church
NO chapel
NO cross
NO cathedral
NO castle
NO fortress
NO coins
NO food
NO wheat
NO stone resources
NO hammer
NO tools
NO workers
NO construction scene
NO second hand
NO text
NO letters
NO numbers
NO heraldic shield
NO decorative symbols

======================================================================
FINAL GOAL
======================================================================

The finished asset should immediately read as:

A REAL RED WAX MEDIEVAL SEAL REPRESENTING THE DONATION OF A BUILDING.

The central idea must be unmistakable:

ONE OPEN SUPPORTING HAND
+
ONE COMPLETE MEDIEVAL BUILDING RESTING IN THE PALM.

The image should communicate:

GIVING A BUILDING AWAY

rather than:

building something,
owning property,
protecting a house,
or constructing a structure.

Natural medieval object first.
Physical wax impression second.
Graphic simplification only where necessary for small-size readability.

NOT a modern charity icon.
NOT a house logo.
NOT a building pasted onto a hand.
NOT a fantasy medallion.
