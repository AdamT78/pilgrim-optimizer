<!-- reference: the light-grey TAKE TITHE wheat seal, named rather than attached -->

Use the previously established light-gray Wheat resource seal as the visual reference for material, realism, lighting, seal construction, thin perimeter, and level of detail.

Create ONE hyper-realistic medieval light gray seal, isolated on a fully transparent background.

SUBJECT
The seal represents the resource:

STONE

The central impression must show ONE substantial piece of rough quarried stone.

It should look like an actual chunk of medieval building stone translated into a physical seal impression — not a modern stone icon, geometric cube, jewel, logo, pictogram, or separate sculpted object placed on top of the seal.

The stone should have a strong, immediately readable silhouette suitable for display at approximately 55–70 px wide.

======================================================================
STONE MOTIF
======================================================================

Show ONE roughly rectangular piece of quarried stone.

It should feel partially worked by a medieval mason but still visibly natural and rough.

The stone should have:

- an approximately block-like overall form
- irregular broken corners
- several broad fractured planes
- a slightly uneven upper surface
- one or two restrained natural fissures
- subtle chisel or splitting evidence
- believable stone mass and weight
- asymmetry and imperfection

The form should remain SIMPLE.

Use approximately 5–7 major planes rather than many tiny facets.

The outer silhouette is more important than surface detail.

The stone should fill approximately 60–68% of the usable seal face.

It should be centered, with enough empty seal surface around it to keep the shape readable.

Do NOT include multiple stones.
Do NOT include a pile of rubble.
Do NOT include a hammer.
Do NOT include a chisel.
Do NOT include a pickaxe.
Do NOT include masonry walls.
Do NOT include an arch.
Do NOT include a building.
Do NOT include mountains.
Do NOT include crystals or gemstones.
Do NOT include text, numbers, letters, shields, crowns, or secondary symbols.

======================================================================
REAL SEAL CONSTRUCTION — VERY IMPORTANT
======================================================================

This must look like a genuine physical seal created by pressing an engraved matrix into the material.

The stone motif must be physically integrated into the seal surface as shallow bas-relief.

It should have:

- rounded compressed relief
- slightly softened edges from the pressing process
- modest variation in relief depth
- broad sculpted planes
- shallow recesses around the stone
- tiny imperfections where the impression did not transfer perfectly

The motif must NOT look like a miniature 3D rock glued onto a circular disc.

The seal face and stone motif must clearly be the SAME continuous material.

Think of an engraved medieval seal matrix depicting a stone block, translated into a convincing physical impression.

======================================================================
IMPORTANT — RELIEF DEPTH
======================================================================

Keep the relief comparatively shallow.

The stone should project only enough to create clear light and shadow.

Avoid exaggerated 3D depth.

Avoid the appearance of a free-standing cube sitting on the seal.

The front-facing planes may be slightly raised, but the entire motif should remain unmistakably part of an impressed seal.

======================================================================
COLOR / MATERIAL
======================================================================

The entire seal must be LIGHT GRAY.

Match the pale warm gray / weathered silver-gray character of the established Wheat resource seal.

Use:

- pale warm stone gray on raised areas
- medium warm gray across the main seal face
- slightly darker gray inside recesses
- restrained off-white highlights where light catches raised material

Avoid pure white.
Avoid blue-gray.
Avoid charcoal black except in the deepest tiny recesses.
Avoid metallic chrome.
Avoid red, brown, gold, green, blue, or other colors.

The material should feel tactile and aged, somewhere between pale sealing compound, stone-gray wax, and an old physical seal — not polished metal.

======================================================================
VERY THIN OUTER EDGE
======================================================================

The seal perimeter must remain VERY THIN, matching the recent Wheat seal direction.

The central usable seal face should occupy approximately 88–92% of the overall diameter.

Around it, leave only a narrow irregular outer lip approximately 4–6% of the seal radius.

The edge should look naturally displaced by the pressing process:

- thin
- slightly uneven
- organic
- gently flattened
- subtly wavy
- physically believable

NO thick scalloped fantasy rim.
NO chunky medallion border.
NO raised decorative ring.
NO rope edge.
NO ornamental frame.
NO coin-like rim.

The silhouette should be nearly circular but naturally imperfect.

======================================================================
SURFACE / AGE
======================================================================

Keep the main seal surface relatively quiet so the Stone resource remains readable.

Add restrained physical imperfections:

- tiny pits
- faint scratches
- subtle compression marks
- a few very fine hairline cracks
- slight variations in surface sheen
- mild edge wear

Do not cover the seal with cracks.

Do not make it look shattered, archaeological, ruined, or heavily damaged.

The motif itself may have one or two visible fractures, but these should reinforce the feeling of natural quarried stone rather than create visual noise.

======================================================================
SMALL-SIZE READABILITY
======================================================================

This asset will often appear only approximately 55–70 px wide.

Design specifically for that size.

At thumbnail scale the player should immediately perceive:

STONE

Therefore:

- use one large object
- preserve a bold block-like silhouette
- exaggerate only the major fractured planes slightly
- avoid small rubble
- avoid microscopic stone texture
- avoid numerous cracks
- avoid thin decorative marks
- maintain strong separation between raised motif and recessed seal face

When reduced dramatically, the image should still read as one rough quarried stone block.

======================================================================
LIGHTING
======================================================================

Soft directional studio lighting from the upper left.

Use lighting primarily to describe the shallow relief.

Use:

- soft highlights along the upper-left edges of the stone
- gentle shadow along lower-right relief edges
- slightly darker recesses between major fractured planes
- restrained specular response

No dramatic spotlight.
No glow.
No magical effects.
No firelight.
No colored reflections.
No external cast shadow.

======================================================================
CAMERA / PRESENTATION
======================================================================

Perfectly front-facing seal.

Orthographic / extremely long focal-length appearance.

No perspective distortion.

Seal centered in the image.

Generous transparent space surrounding the complete seal.

Fully transparent background outside the seal.

No white background.
No black background.
No parchment.
No tabletop.
No surface underneath.
No frame.
No external shadow.

Production-quality board-game UI asset.

======================================================================
FINAL GOAL
======================================================================

The finished object should immediately read as:

A REALISTIC LIGHT-GRAY MEDIEVAL RESOURCE SEAL WITH ONE ROUGH-HEWN QUARRIED STONE BLOCK IMPRESSED INTO ITS SURFACE.

It should belong visually to the exact same resource family as the light-gray Wheat seal.

Natural object first.
Physical seal impression second.
Graphic simplification only where required for small-size readability.

NOT a stone icon.
NOT a rock pasted onto a medallion.
NOT a masonry symbol.
NOT a construction-action symbol.
NOT a fantasy coin.
