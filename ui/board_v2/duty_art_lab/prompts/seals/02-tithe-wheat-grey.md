reference image: produce wheat
Use the attached image as the primary reference for motif style, realism, surface treatment, and overall seal construction.

Create ONE hyper-realistic medieval light gray seal, isolated on a fully transparent background.

SUBJECT
The seal represents WHEAT.

The central impression must show only ONE mature wheat stalk, based closely on the motif language of the attached image.

IMPORTANT:
- Keep the same naturalistic sculpted style as the attached image
- Keep the same kind of realistic wheat-ear modelling
- Keep the same believable stem-and-grain construction
- Keep the same physical "pressed into seal" feeling
- But reduce the design to a SINGLE wheat stalk only

The single wheat stalk should:
- be centered
- be upright
- have a slender natural stem
- have one clearly formed wheat ear
- show individually suggested grains / spikelets
- include a few restrained awns
- feel botanical and realistic, not symbolic or icon-like
- remain simple enough to read clearly at small UI sizes

Do NOT include three stalks.
Do NOT include a bundle.
Do NOT include extra decorative wheat around it.
Do NOT include any other symbols.

======================================================================
REAL SEAL CONSTRUCTION — VERY IMPORTANT
======================================================================

This must look like a genuine physical seal with the wheat motif impressed into the surface.

The wheat must feel like part of the seal itself:
- shallow bas-relief impression
- rounded compressed forms
- slightly softened edges
- subtle variation in impression depth
- realistic shallow recesses around the wheat
- small natural imperfections from the pressing process

Do NOT make the wheat look like a separate ornament glued on top.
Do NOT make it look like a printed icon.
Do NOT make it look like a modern logo.

======================================================================
COLOR / MATERIAL
======================================================================

The seal must be LIGHT GRAY.

Use a pale stone-gray / ash-gray / soft weathered silver-gray palette.

It should read as a light gray physical seal, with:
- gentle tonal variation
- slightly darker gray in recesses
- subtle highlights on raised areas
- a believable tactile surface

Avoid bright white.
Avoid strong blue-gray.
Avoid red.
Avoid gold.
Avoid colorful reflections.

The final object should feel like a realistic light gray seal, suitable for a medieval dark-fantasy board-game UI asset.

======================================================================
VERY THIN OUTER EDGE
======================================================================

The outer edge of the seal must remain THIN.

Avoid thick chunky fantasy-medallion borders.

The central impressed area should occupy roughly 88–92% of the seal diameter.

Around it, leave only a narrow irregular outer lip.

The edge should be:
- thin
- organic
- slightly uneven
- subtly wavy
- physically believable

NO thick rim.
NO decorative circular frame.
NO rope border.
NO ornamental ring.
NO coin-like edge.
NO large scalloped lobes.

The silhouette should be nearly circular, but naturally imperfect.

======================================================================
SURFACE / AGE
======================================================================

Add restrained realism:
- tiny pits
- faint scratches
- slight wear
- a few subtle hairline cracks if needed
- minute gloss variation or surface sheen depending on material

Keep damage restrained.
The seal should look aged and handled, but intact and elegant.

======================================================================
LIGHTING
======================================================================

Soft directional lighting from upper left.

The lighting must reveal the relief naturally.

Use:
- small soft highlights
- gentle shadows in recesses
- clear readability of the single wheat stalk

No dramatic glow.
No magical lighting.
No firelight.
No colored light.

======================================================================
CAMERA / PRESENTATION
======================================================================

Perfectly front-facing seal.

Orthographic / extremely long focal-length appearance.
No perspective distortion.

Seal centered in the image.

Generous transparent space around the seal.

Fully transparent background outside the seal.
No cast shadow outside the object.
No parchment.
No surface beneath it.
No frame.

======================================================================
FINAL GOAL
======================================================================

The finished object should read immediately as:

A hyper-realistic light gray medieval seal with a single realistic wheat-stalk impression, using the same motif language and sculpted realism as the attached reference image, but simplified from three stalks down to one.
