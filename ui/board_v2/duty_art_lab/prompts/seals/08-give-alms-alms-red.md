Do not reinterpret, redesign, restyle, reposition, simplify, enlarge, shrink, rotate, or anatomically alter the hand.

KEEP THE EXISTING HAND

REPLACE THE BUILDING WITH ONE COIN + ONE WHEAT STALK
KEEP THE SAME GENERAL SEAL CONSTRUCTION

Also use the attached light-gray coin image as the primary reference for the COIN MOTIF ONLY — specifically its hammered medieval coin shape, irregular edge, worn character, and central broad-armed cross design.

Do NOT copy the light-gray coin's color or material.

Create ONE hyper-realistic medieval RED WAX SEAL, isolated on a fully transparent background.

======================================================================
SUBJECT
======================================================================

The seal represents:

GIVE ALMS / DONATE RESOURCES

SILVER + WHEAT

The central impression must show:

ONE OPEN SUPPORTING HAND
+

+
ONE MATURE WHEAT STALK

SILVER AND WHEAT BEING GIVEN / OFFERED / DONATED

rather than wealth, harvest, taxation, trade, production, or storage.

======================================================================
THE HAND — LOCKED REFERENCE ELEMENT
======================================================================

THIS IS ONE OF THE MOST IMPORTANT REQUIREMENTS.

Reproduce the hand from the attached red seal reference as faithfully as possible.

Match the reference hand in:

- exact overall silhouette
- exact orientation
- exact palm angle
- exact wrist position
- exact cuff position and width
- exact thumb shape and position
- exact finger count
- exact finger lengths
- exact finger spacing
- exact fingertip curve
- exact upward bend of the fingers
- exact relationship between palm, thumb and fingers
- exact overall width
- exact vertical position
- exact horizontal position
- exact scale relative to the seal
- exact shallow-relief treatment

The hand should occupy essentially the SAME PIXEL FOOTPRINT / PROPORTIONAL FOOTPRINT within the seal as in the attached reference.

Do NOT create a "similar" hand.

Do NOT create a new interpretation of an offering hand.

Do NOT make the hand more anatomical.

Do NOT change finger curvature.

Do NOT open or close the hand further.

Do NOT change the cuff.

Do NOT change the wrist.

Do NOT rotate the hand.

Do NOT mirror the hand.

Do NOT move the hand downward to create room.

Do NOT shrink the hand to fit the resources.

Instead, design the coin and wheat around the EXISTING HAND GEOMETRY.

Imagine that the original wax matrix remains completely unchanged in the hand area and ONLY the building portion of the matrix has been recut.

======================================================================
RESOURCE PLACEMENT
======================================================================

Remove the building from the reference composition.

In the space previously occupied by the building, place:

1. ONE medieval hammered silver coin motif
2. ONE mature wheat stalk / wheat ear

Both should appear clearly supported by the SAME unchanged palm.

The resources should visually rest immediately above the hand.

Do not place them too high above the palm.

Do not leave an unnatural floating gap.

Do not make them look balanced precariously on individual fingers.

The composition should feel like the hand is deliberately presenting both resources.

Suggested placement:

LEFT / UPPER-LEFT:
one medieval coin

RIGHT / UPPER-RIGHT:
one mature wheat stalk

BOTTOM:
the exact hand from the attached reference

Keep the grouping compact.

======================================================================
SILVER COIN — MATCH ATTACHED COIN MOTIF
======================================================================

Show ONE substantial medieval hammered coin.

Use the attached light-gray coin image as the motif reference.

Match its essential design:

- irregular hand-struck circular shape
- worn chipped perimeter
- slightly uneven medieval coin surface
- broad simple central cross
- same general cross proportions
- same broad flared arms
- same plain austere appearance
- no surrounding inscription
- no extra ornament

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT render actual silver metal.

Do NOT make it gray.

Do NOT make it a separate physical coin sitting on the wax.

It must be represented as an impression within the red wax matrix.

Use one coin only.

NO stacked coins.
NO second coin.
NO loose coins.
NO money bag.
NO chest.
NO scales.

======================================================================

- one clear slender stem

- individually suggested grains / spikelets
- a few restrained awns
- slight natural asymmetry
- clear botanical structure
- broad enough forms to remain readable at small size

Do NOT use:

- multiple wheat stalks
- a sheaf
- a bundle
- bread
- grain sacks
- baskets
- fields
- farming tools

Use ONE wheat stalk only.

======================================================================
REAL WAX-SEAL CONSTRUCTION
======================================================================

This must look like a genuine physical red wax seal made by pressing an engraved metal matrix into warm sealing wax.

The hand, coin, and wheat must all appear as shallow bas-relief impressions in ONE continuous piece of wax.

Everything is the same red wax material.

Use:

- shallow rounded relief
- softly compressed edges
- modest impression depth
- subtle recesses separating forms
- tiny imperfect transfer marks
- physically believable wax compression

Do NOT make anything look glued on.

Do NOT make the coin look like an actual coin laid on top.

Do NOT make the wheat look like a separate miniature.

Do NOT create an embossed modern icon.

======================================================================
RELIEF DEPTH
======================================================================

Keep the relief similar to the attached red hand/building seal.

The HAND relief should specifically match the reference.

The new coin and wheat should use comparable relief depth so that they look as though they came from the same engraved matrix.

Avoid deep sculptural projection.

Avoid strong undercuts.

Avoid floating forms.

- brighter crimson highlights on raised forms
- deeper burgundy in shallow recesses
- restrained polished wax highlights
- subtle translucency only along very thin edges
- minor natural gloss variation

Everything must remain RED WAX.
