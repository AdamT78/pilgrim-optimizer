reference image: Tithe resource wheat

Use the attached image as the visual reference for material, realism, lighting, shallow relief, very thin perimeter, and small-size readability.

Create ONE hyper-realistic medieval LIGHT GRAY seal, isolated on a fully transparent background.

SUBJECT
The seal represents the resource:

SILVER / COIN

The central impression must show ONE SINGLE medieval silver coin.

The coin should look like an actual hand-struck medieval coin translated into a physical seal impression — not a modern currency icon, generic vector circle, casino chip, medallion, logo, or separate metal object placed on top of the seal.

It must remain immediately recognizable as ONE COIN when displayed at approximately 55–70 px wide.

======================================================================
COIN MOTIF
======================================================================

Show ONE substantial medieval hammered coin.

The coin should be:

- broadly circular but slightly irregular
- subtly uneven from hand striking
- slightly thicker-looking than a flat disc
- gently tilted or turned by approximately 10–15° so a small amount of its edge is visible
- worn and imperfect
- simple enough to read clearly at small size

The coin should occupy approximately 58–65% of the usable seal face.

Keep it centered.

The design on the coin itself should be extremely restrained.

Use ONE simple medieval mark such as:

- a plain cross
OR
- a simple quatrefoil
OR
- a very simple geometric mint mark

Keep this symbol shallow and broad.

The central mark is secondary.
The object must first and foremost read as:

A SINGLE SILVER COIN.

Do NOT include:
- multiple coins
- stacked coins
- scattered coins
- a money bag
- a treasure chest
- scales
- hands
- crowns
- elaborate heraldry
- text
- letters
- numbers
- detailed inscriptions
- decorative borders around the coin

======================================================================
MEDIEVAL COIN REALISM
======================================================================

The coin should feel like a genuine medieval hammered coin rather than a modern machine-minted coin.

Include restrained characteristics such as:

- slightly irregular circular outline
- subtly uneven thickness
- mild asymmetry
- gently worn edges
- shallow hammering irregularities
- faint dents
- slightly off-centre impression if natural
- modest surface wear

Do NOT make the coin perfectly machined.

Do NOT use modern milled or ridged edges.

Do NOT make it look pristine or newly manufactured.

======================================================================
REAL SEAL CONSTRUCTION — VERY IMPORTANT
======================================================================

This must look like a genuine physical seal created by pressing an engraved matrix into the material.

The coin motif must be physically integrated into the seal surface as shallow bas-relief.

The coin and the surrounding seal must clearly be the SAME continuous light-gray material.

The motif should have:

- rounded compressed relief
- slightly softened edges
- subtle variation in impression depth
- shallow recessed separation around the coin
- broad readable planes
- tiny imperfections caused by the pressing process

The coin must NOT look like an actual loose metal coin glued onto the seal.

It must NOT look like a separate object resting on top.

It must look like:

A COIN DEPICTED BY THE IMPRESSION OF A MEDIEVAL SEAL MATRIX.

======================================================================
IMPORTANT — RELIEF DEPTH
======================================================================

Keep the relief shallow.

The coin should project only enough to create clear light and shadow.

Its visible thickness should be suggested through the engraved relief, not through exaggerated 3D projection.

Avoid making the coin look like a thick puck.

Avoid deep undercuts.

Avoid exaggerated perspective.

The entire motif should remain unmistakably part of the seal face.

======================================================================
COLOR / MATERIAL
======================================================================

The entire seal must be LIGHT GRAY.

Match the established resource-seal palette:

- pale warm stone-gray highlights
- medium warm gray main surface
- slightly darker gray recesses
- restrained off-white highlights on raised areas

Avoid pure white.
Avoid blue-gray.
Avoid black except in the deepest tiny recesses.
Avoid chrome.
Avoid strongly metallic silver reflections.
Avoid red, brown, gold, green, or blue.

The result should visually belong directly beside the existing light-gray Wheat resource seal.

The material should feel tactile and aged — somewhere between pale sealing compound, weathered silver-gray wax, and an old physical seal.

======================================================================
VERY THIN OUTER EDGE
======================================================================

The seal perimeter must be VERY THIN.

The central usable seal face should occupy approximately 88–92% of the total diameter.

Around it, leave only a narrow irregular outer lip approximately 4–6% of the seal radius.

The edge should appear naturally displaced by the pressing process:

- thin
- organic
- gently flattened
- slightly uneven
- subtly wavy
- physically believable

NO thick fantasy rim.
NO chunky scalloped border.
NO decorative ring.
NO rope edge.
NO ornamental frame.
NO coin-like outer border.

IMPORTANT:
The OUTER SEAL must not itself resemble a giant coin.

The single coin motif in the centre must remain clearly distinguishable from the thin irregular seal perimeter.

======================================================================
SURFACE / AGE
======================================================================

Keep the main seal face quiet and restrained.

Add only subtle physical imperfections:

- tiny pits
- faint scratches
- light compression marks
- occasional very fine hairline cracks
- minute variation in surface sheen
- slight edge wear

Keep damage limited.

The seal should look aged and handled but intact.

The coin motif itself may have:

- one or two tiny dents
- slight edge wear
- subtle struck-metal irregularity

Do not cover it in scratches or cracks.

======================================================================
SMALL-SIZE READABILITY
======================================================================

This asset will often appear approximately 55–70 px wide.

Design specifically for that size.

At thumbnail scale, the player must immediately perceive:

ONE COIN / SILVER

Therefore:

- use one large coin
- preserve a bold circular silhouette
- make its slight tilt readable
- show a small amount of visible edge thickness
- use one broad central mark at most
- avoid inscriptions
- avoid tiny medieval decorative details
- avoid multiple concentric rings
- avoid excessive texture

When reduced dramatically, it should still unmistakably look like one old silver coin.

======================================================================
LIGHTING
======================================================================

Soft directional studio lighting from the upper left.

Use lighting to reveal the shallow relief:

- gentle highlight on the upper-left coin rim
- subtle shadow beneath the lower-right edge
- slightly darker recessed separation around the coin
- soft highlight on the central medieval mark

No dramatic spotlight.
No glow.
No magical light.
No firelight.
No colored reflections.
No external cast shadow.

======================================================================
CAMERA / PRESENTATION
======================================================================

Perfectly front-facing seal.

Orthographic / extremely long focal-length appearance.

No perspective distortion of the overall seal.

Seal centered in the image.

Generous transparent space around the complete seal.

Fully transparent background outside the seal.

No white background.
No black background.
No parchment.
No tabletop.
No surface underneath.
No frame.
No external shadow.

Production-quality medieval dark-fantasy board-game UI resource asset.

======================================================================
FINAL GOAL
======================================================================

The finished object should immediately read as:

A HYPER-REALISTIC LIGHT-GRAY MEDIEVAL RESOURCE SEAL WITH ONE SINGLE WORN HAMMERED SILVER COIN IMPRESSED INTO ITS SURFACE.

It should belong visually to the exact same resource family as the existing light-gray Wheat seal.

Natural medieval coin first.
Physical seal impression second.
Graphic simplification only where necessary for small-size readability.

NOT stacked coins.
NOT a money symbol.
NOT a modern coin.
NOT a fantasy medallion.
NOT a coin glued onto a seal.
NOT an ornate icon.
