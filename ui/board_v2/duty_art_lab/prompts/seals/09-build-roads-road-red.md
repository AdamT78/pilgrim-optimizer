======================================================================
SUBJECT
======================================================================

The seal represents:

BUILD ROADS

The central impression must show the motif from the attached reference image:

- one winding medieval stone road / cobbled path
- receding upward into the distance
- flanked by two upright roadside marker stones / boundary stones
- each marker stone simple, tall, and readable
- the motif should feel like a medieval road emblem, not a modern road sign

Use the attached image only for the MOTIF COMPOSITION AND MOTIF LANGUAGE:

- the general winding-road layout
- the broad path shape
- the two flanking upright stones
- the overall symbolic road-building concept

Do NOT copy:

- the attached seal's border
- the attached outer rim
- its exact crack pattern
- its exact wax silhouette
- its exact perimeter shape

The motif must remain simple enough to be clearly recognizable when the seal is displayed at approximately 55–70 px wide.

======================================================================
MOTIF DESIGN — ROAD / BUILD ROADS
======================================================================

The central impression should show:

- a single winding paved road
- built from broad stone blocks / cobbles
- wider at the bottom and tapering as it recedes upward
- gently curving rather than perfectly straight
- clearly legible as a constructed road
- two simple upright marker stones / roadside stones positioned one on each side of the road

The road should feel like a medieval built roadway, not a natural dirt path.

The road should have:

- broad readable paving stones
- slightly irregular medieval stonework
- a clear continuous route
- a strong silhouette
- restrained perspective within a mostly front-facing seal motif

The two flanking stones should:

- be simple and bold
- read as roadside markers / boundary stones
- remain subordinate to the road
- help frame the road and reinforce the "build roads" idea

If the roadside stones contain a simple decorative cut or heraldic incision, keep it very restrained and readable, similar in spirit to the reference motif.

Do NOT include:

- wheat
- coins
- tools
- workers
- carts
- horses
- buildings
- castles
- bridges unless implied only subtly through the road construction
- text
- letters
- numbers
- banners
- extra scenery
- trees
- mountains
- sky
- a full landscape scene

This must read as a seal impression motif, not a picture scene.

======================================================================
REAL WAX-SEAL CONSTRUCTION — VERY IMPORTANT
======================================================================

This must look like a genuine physical wax seal made by pressing an engraved metal seal matrix into warm sealing wax.

Use deep crimson / blood-red sealing wax.

The road motif must be physically impressed into the wax as a convincing bas-relief impression.

It should have:

- rounded compressed relief
- slightly softened edges caused by warm wax
- subtle variation in impression depth
- fine compression marks
- realistic shallow recesses around the raised forms
- tiny imperfections in the impression

Do NOT make the road look like a separate object glued onto the wax.

Do NOT make it look like a miniature 3D sculpture sitting on top of the wax.

The road, marker stones, and wax must all be one continuous material.

======================================================================
VERY THIN OUTER EDGE
======================================================================

The wax perimeter must be VERY THIN.

Avoid the thick, chunky, scalloped border commonly seen in fantasy wax-seal illustrations.

The central impressed area should occupy roughly 88–92% of the seal diameter.

Around it, leave only a narrow irregular wax lip approximately 4–6% of the seal radius.

The outside edge should look like a small amount of warm wax naturally displaced outward when the matrix was pressed down.

It should be:

- thin
- slightly uneven
- organic
- gently flattened
- subtly wavy
- physically believable

NO thick raised rim.
NO decorative circular frame.
NO rope border.
NO ornamental ring.
NO coin-like edge.
NO large scalloped lobes.

The silhouette should be nearly circular but naturally imperfect.

IMPORTANT:

Create a NEW border.

Do NOT copy the border shape from the attached reference image.

======================================================================
ROAD IMPRESSION
======================================================================

Make the road motif the dominant feature.

The full road + marker-stone motif should fill approximately 65–72% of the usable seal face.

The winding road should be the strongest shape.

Use broad, readable paving stones rather than excessive microscopic stone detail.

The motif should feel like it could genuinely have been engraved into a medieval metal seal matrix and successfully impressed into wax.

Natural physical structure first; graphic simplification only where required by the seal-making process and small-size readability.

No surrounding wreath.
No crown.
No shield.
No text.
No letters.
No numbers.
No extra symbols beyond the road motif and its two flanking marker stones.

======================================================================
MATERIAL AND AGE
======================================================================

Hyper-realistic dark red beeswax / sealing wax.

Rich crimson red with deeper burgundy in recesses.

Subtle wax translucency only at very thin raised edges.

Small realistic imperfections:

- tiny pits
- faint scratches
- very small hairline cracks
- slight compression marks
- minute variation in gloss

Keep these restrained.

The seal should look aged and handled but intact, not shattered or heavily cracked.

The surface should remain predominantly smooth wax rather than stone, metal, ceramic, or resin.

======================================================================
LIGHTING
======================================================================

Soft directional studio lighting from upper left.

The lighting must reveal the shallow relief of the road motif naturally.

Use:

- small specular highlights on polished wax
- soft but readable shadows inside recesses
- enough contrast to make the winding road and paving blocks clear at small size

No dramatic glow.
No magical lighting.
No firelight.
No coloured reflections.

No perspective distortion.

Seal centred in the image.

No cast shadow outside the object.
No surface beneath it.

Production-quality board-game UI asset.
