reference image: Tithe resource stone

Use the previously established Stone resource seal as the structural reference for realism, shallow relief, lighting, thin perimeter, and small-size readability.

Create ONE hyper-realistic medieval RED WAX SEAL, isolated on a fully transparent background.

SUBJECT
The seal represents:

MORE STONE / INCREASED STONE PRODUCTION

It should clearly belong to the same visual family as the existing Stone resource seal, but communicate a GREATER AMOUNT or RICHER YIELD of stone.

Do NOT communicate this merely by showing several identical stone blocks.

Instead, show ONE dominant rough-hewn quarried stone in the foreground, backed by the suggestion of a larger exposed stone mass or freshly split quarry face.

The result should immediately feel like:

MORE STONE AVAILABLE

rather than simply:

A DIFFERENT STONE ICON.

======================================================================
STONE MOTIF
======================================================================

The central motif should consist of:

- ONE large rough-hewn rectangular stone block as the primary object
- behind it, a broad broken quarry face / exposed stone mass
- a few large fractured planes suggesting that substantial material remains available
- restrained splitting scars and natural fissures
- possibly two or three very small broken chips near the base, but these must remain subordinate

The main foreground stone should be visually similar to the existing Stone resource motif:
- substantial
- partially worked
- broad fractured surfaces
- irregular corners
- believable medieval building stone
- heavy and dense

The quarry mass behind it should NOT look like a mountain landscape.

It should instead read as a compressed heraldic-style representation of a freshly exposed stone seam or larger rough block from which the foreground stone has been separated.

Think:

ONE FINISHED QUARRIED BLOCK + A LARGE RESERVE OF STONE BEHIND IT.

This communicates abundance without simply multiplying blocks.

======================================================================
COMPOSITION
======================================================================

Keep the motif bold and simple.

The foreground block should occupy approximately 45–50% of the usable seal width.

The broader quarry mass behind it should bring the total stone motif to approximately 68–72% of the usable seal face.

Create a simple depth hierarchy:

BACK:
broad rough fractured stone mass

FRONT:
one clearly readable quarried block

Do not create a realistic landscape scene.

Do not include perspective-heavy cliffs or mountains.

The motif must still function like an engraved medieval seal impression.

At approximately 55–70 px wide, the player should perceive:

A LARGE QUANTITY OF STONE

with the foreground block remaining the strongest recognizable shape.

======================================================================
REAL WAX-SEAL CONSTRUCTION — VERY IMPORTANT
======================================================================

This must look like a genuine physical red wax seal created by pressing an engraved metal matrix into warm sealing wax.

The entire stone motif must be physically impressed into the wax as shallow bas-relief.

The foreground block and quarry mass must be made from the SAME RED WAX as the seal.

They are NOT actual gray stones sitting on red wax.

They should have:

- shallow rounded relief
- softened pressed edges
- modest variation in impression depth
- broad sculpted planes
- recessed separation between major stone surfaces
- tiny imperfect areas where the matrix did not press completely cleanly

Everything must feel like ONE continuous wax object.

Do NOT make the stone look glued onto the seal.
Do NOT make it look like a miniature 3D rock placed on top.
Do NOT make it look like a printed icon.

======================================================================
RED WAX MATERIAL
======================================================================

Use deep crimson / blood-red sealing wax.

Rich dark crimson across the seal face.

Use:
- slightly brighter crimson highlights on raised relief
- deeper burgundy in recesses
- subtle wax translucency only at very thin edges
- restrained polished highlights
- natural variation in gloss

The stone motif must remain red wax.

Do NOT make the stone gray.
Do NOT introduce brown, blue, green, gold, or metallic colors.

The distinction between stone surfaces must come from RELIEF, LIGHT and SHADOW rather than different colors.

======================================================================
VERY THIN OUTER EDGE
======================================================================

Keep the outer wax perimeter VERY THIN.

The central usable seal face should occupy approximately 88–92% of the total diameter.

Around it, leave only a narrow irregular wax lip approximately 4–6% of the seal radius.

The outer edge should look like warm wax naturally displaced when the seal matrix was pressed.

It should be:

- thin
- irregular
- organic
- gently flattened
- subtly wavy
- physically believable

NO thick fantasy rim.
NO scalloped medallion border.
NO decorative circular frame.
NO rope ring.
NO coin edge.
NO ornamental surround.

======================================================================
ABUNDANCE WITHOUT CLUTTER
======================================================================

The impression must communicate MORE STONE primarily through SCALE and MASS.

Use:

- a larger background stone reserve
- broad exposed fractured surfaces
- one substantial foreground block
- overlapping mass
- strong visual weight

Do NOT solve "more stone" by filling the image with many small rocks.

Avoid:
- piles of identical cubes
- rubble heaps
- dozens of fragments
- tiny stones
- busy quarry scenes
- mining carts
- workers
- tools

The visual idea should be:

A large reserve has been quarried, and one substantial block has already been produced.

======================================================================
SMALL-SIZE READABILITY
======================================================================

The seal will often be shown approximately 55–70 px wide.

Design specifically for this.

Use only a few broad shapes.

The foreground block must have a bold silhouette.

The quarry mass behind it should form one clear secondary silhouette.

Limit the stone motif to approximately 6–9 major visible planes total.

Avoid microscopic texture.

Avoid excessive cracking.

Avoid thin engraved lines.

When dramatically reduced, the image must still clearly communicate:

STONE — IN GREATER QUANTITY.

======================================================================
SURFACE / AGE
======================================================================

The seal itself should show restrained physical age:

- tiny pits
- faint scratches
- very small hairline cracks
- subtle compression marks
- slight variations in wax sheen
- mild edge wear

Keep damage restrained.

The seal should feel old and handled but intact.

Do not make it shattered or archaeological.

======================================================================
LIGHTING
======================================================================

Soft directional studio lighting from upper left.

Use the light to clarify the broad relief:

- soft highlights along upper-left relief edges
- slightly darker burgundy on lower-right edges
- deeper shadow where foreground stone overlaps the reserve behind
- subtle recessed shadows between major fractured planes

No magical lighting.
No glow.
No firelight.
No colored reflections.
No external cast shadow.

======================================================================
CAMERA / PRESENTATION
======================================================================

Perfectly front-facing.

Orthographic / extremely long focal-length appearance.

No perspective distortion.

Seal centered.

Generous transparent space around the complete seal.

Fully transparent background outside the seal.

No black background.
No white background.
No parchment.
No tabletop.
No frame.
No external shadow.

Production-quality medieval dark-fantasy board-game UI asset.

======================================================================
FINAL GOAL
======================================================================

The finished asset should immediately read as:

A REAL RED WAX RESOURCE SEAL REPRESENTING A LARGE YIELD OF QUARRIED STONE.

It should visually relate directly to the simpler Stone resource seal, but communicate increased quantity through:

ONE LARGE QUARRIED BLOCK
+
A BROADER EXPOSED STONE RESERVE BEHIND IT.

NOT several identical blocks.
NOT a rubble pile.
NOT a mountain.
NOT a construction symbol.
NOT mining equipment.
NOT a fantasy coin.
