Create ONE hyper-realistic medieval red wax seal, isolated on a fully transparent background.

SUBJECT
The seal represents WHEAT.

The central impression must show a simple, naturalistic motif of real wheat:
three mature wheat stalks gathered loosely together, with the central stalk slightly taller than the two outer stalks.

The wheat must look like actual wheat translated into a physical wax impression — NOT like a modern icon, logo, pictogram, vector symbol, clip-art graphic, heraldic badge, or a symbol simply placed on top of the wax.

Each wheat stalk should have:
- a believable slender stem
- a clearly formed wheat ear
- individually suggested grains / spikelets
- a few restrained awns
- subtle natural asymmetry
- slightly different angles between the three stalks

The motif should remain simple enough to be clearly recognizable when the seal is displayed at approximately 55–70 px wide.

======================================================================
REAL WAX-SEAL CONSTRUCTION — VERY IMPORTANT
======================================================================

This must look like a genuine physical wax seal made by pressing an engraved metal seal matrix into warm sealing wax.

Deep crimson / blood-red sealing wax.

The wheat motif is physically impressed into the wax as a convincing bas-relief impression.

It should have:
- rounded compressed relief
- slightly softened edges caused by warm wax
- tiny imperfections in the impression
- subtle variation in impression depth
- very fine compression marks
- realistic shallow recesses around raised wheat forms

Do NOT make the wheat look like a separate object glued onto the seal.
Do NOT make it look like a 3D metal ornament sitting on the wax.
The wheat and wax must be one continuous material.

======================================================================
VERY THIN OUTER EDGE
======================================================================

The wax perimeter must be VERY THIN.

Avoid the thick, chunky, scalloped border commonly seen in fantasy wax-seal illustrations.

The central impressed area should occupy roughly 88–92% of the seal diameter.

Around it, leave only a narrow irregular wax lip approximately 4–6% of the seal radius.

The outside edge should look like a small amount of warm wax naturally displaced outward when the matrix was pressed down.

It should be:
- thin
- slightly uneven
- organic
- gently flattened
- subtly wavy
- physically believable

NO thick raised rim.
NO decorative circular frame.
NO rope border.
NO ornamental ring.
NO coin-like edge.
NO large scalloped lobes.

The silhouette should be nearly circular but naturally imperfect.

======================================================================
WHEAT IMPRESSION
======================================================================

Make the wheat the dominant feature.

The three stalks should fill approximately 65–70% of the usable seal face.

Use relatively broad, readable forms rather than excessive microscopic detail.

Individual grains should be suggested through sculpted form and shadow, not drawn as fine lines.

The motif should feel like it could genuinely have been engraved into a medieval metal seal matrix and successfully impressed into wax.

Natural botanical structure first; graphic simplification only where required by the physical seal-making process.

No surrounding wreath.
No crown.
No shield.
No text.
No letters.
No numbers.
No additional symbols.

======================================================================
MATERIAL AND AGE
======================================================================

Hyper-realistic dark red beeswax / sealing wax.

Rich crimson red with deeper burgundy in recesses.

Subtle wax translucency only at very thin raised edges.

Small realistic imperfections:
- tiny pits
- faint scratches
- very small hairline cracks
- slight compression marks
- minute variation in gloss

Keep these restrained.

The seal should look aged and handled but intact, not shattered or heavily cracked.

The surface should remain predominantly smooth wax rather than stone, metal, ceramic, or resin.

======================================================================
LIGHTING
======================================================================

Soft directional studio lighting from upper left.

The lighting must reveal the shallow relief of the wheat naturally.

Small specular highlights on polished wax.
Deep but soft shadows inside the impressed recesses.

No dramatic glow.
No magical lighting.
No firelight.
No coloured reflections.

======================================================================
CAMERA / PRESENTATION
======================================================================

Perfectly front-facing seal.

Orthographic / extremely long focal length appearance.
No perspective distortion.

Seal centred in the image.

Generous transparent space around the seal.

Fully transparent background outside the seal.
No cast shadow outside the object.
No surface beneath it.
No parchment.
No black background.
No frame.

Production-quality board-game UI asset.

The finished object should immediately read as:
A REAL RED WAX SEAL WITH A REALISTIC THREE-STALK WHEAT IMPRESSION.

Not an icon printed onto wax.
Not a medallion.
Not a fantasy coin.
Not a sculpted wheat ornament attached to a disc.
