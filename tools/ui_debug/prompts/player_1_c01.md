### MATCH THE OTHER GAME PIECES

Render him as an **unpainted tabletop board-game sculpt** with the same sculptural
language and level of detail as the hooded pilgrim/monk miniature from this game:

- solid opaque single-colour resin/plastic
- smooth matte surface
- clearly sculpted, rounded forms
- crisp but not razor-sharp edges
- substantial robe folds that remain readable when the piece is small
- restrained surface detail rather than photorealistic fabric texture
- facial features sculpted like a high-quality physical miniature, not like a photograph
- the bare head and face are the same unpainted material as the robe, with the same
  smooth matte surface: skin and cloth are one substance, not two
- the scalp is bald: one continuous smooth surface from brow to nape, the same
  unpainted material and matte finish as the robe
- the eyebrows are sculpted masses with carved edges
- age reads in the planes and hollows of the face, cut at the same scale as the robe
  folds beside it
- no skin colour, makeup, fabric colours or painted details
- no metallic material

Keep him tall and upright.

### CLOTHING

Take the design from the second image:

- long medieval monastic habit reaching the ground
- wide hanging sleeves
- heavy hood and shoulder mantle, worn DOWN so the head is bare

Simplify tiny folds where necessary so the miniature reads clearly at board-game
scale, but keep the same overall drapery and costume.
