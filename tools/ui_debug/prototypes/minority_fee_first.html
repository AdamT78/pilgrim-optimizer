Paying the minority fee first

A minority on a Duty tile costs 1 silver before the action does anything. The engine has always charged it; no duty has ever named it. These states put it in its own step, ahead of everything else, from the real payload of bank_hire_market_construct_substitution_001. The map and player board are the app's own, lifted from the running page — the building outlines are the real choice keys, revealed and hidden as the page reveals them.

What the engine already does

duty_value_and_silver_cost(strength) takes strength alone — no duty category — so minority means duty value 1 and 1 silver on every duty. Measured: 39 minority resolutions across the corpus, 39 charged; no non-minority resolution ever charged. A tithe never charges it.

What this changes

Not the money. A Mint still costs a silver and a stone. What changes is that each is attached to the rule that charges it, rather than arriving as an unexplained Cost 2 on a one-stone building — which is exactly what sent you hunting for a bug.

The load-bearing change underneath is that the action's own cost must now EXCLUDE the fee. _resource_payment_costs reads RESOURCE_DELTA, which includes the minority silver today, so the payment step currently absorbs it. The invariant to hold is fee + action cost == what the game actually takes, checked by applying the action and diffing resources rather than by reading the payload's own account of the cost.

State 6 is the one the engine fix just made possible. Before it, a minority player without silver was offered no duty at all.
