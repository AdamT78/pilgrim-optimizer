Allocation, Clerical, Ordination — nine states

5 tiles with pictures, 4 of them with a label in the board's hover gold; 2 marked. The rest carry pieces only, in the seat colours dimmed.

This prototype does not include graphical design or production pictures, instead it tries to explain a game turn for Player 3.

In this prototype acolutes are represented by colored rectangles in order to a) save space and b) make the reading of the board easier. This design does not cover over any art as the current design does when acolytes are added to Duty tiles. However, this prototype needs logic for when more than 4 acolytes are present (i.e., limit 3 or 4 plus number).

The Idea is to contain all text and decisions to the selected duty tile after the Sow phase, i.e., this replace the corresponding section of the action board. Also, all clickable actions are gathered “in one place”. However, with many acolytes for all players in a 4 player game (extremely unusual / unlikely), the Tithe resource on the Board can look a bit obscured, but don’t think this is a big issue.

Currently, the Merchant is not included in this prototype.
