What is new here, and what it costs

An owned Bank needs no hire question: the total is just the move count, so the first pill click can both settle the payment and end the move phase. A hired Bank breaks that, because the hire fee is part of the total. Hence the extra step at state 2 — and it, rather than the first pill, is what ends the move phase.

Where only one path is affordable there is no question to ask, and the panel says what is happening instead of asking it (state 7). That mirrors the owned-Bank rule that a single affordable mix is still clicked out by hand rather than auto-applied.

Three known defects live on this path today and are what the branch would fix: the hire sentence appears while you are still lifting acolytes, from the sharedHireText abstention bug at play_view_turn.js:1796; the label reads 2 silver, hires the Bank for a two-move spend that is really 2 silver and 1 wheat; and payment is still buttons in the turn column rather than pills on the board.
